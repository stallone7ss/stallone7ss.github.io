Our story

From a 2022 idea to a registered Milne Bay business

The idea for KILSTA was conceived in 2022 by men from Milne Bay who had watched local businesses and organisations struggle to find dependable Business and ICT support in the province. Two years later, in 2024, that idea became a registered sole proprietorship — KILSTA Business & ICT Consultants.

Our staff have returned to Alotau to pursue this opportunity, bringing experience from other business, ICT and education organisations. We are a young, newly registered company, and our aim is simple: serve our clients well, grow steadily from small to medium scale, and open as many job opportunities and career paths as possible for young people from our local communities and the province.

Locally owned and run

KILSTA is 100% owned and run by men from Milne Bay. We operate a centralised project management model from a home-based office, with plans to establish a central office in Alotau town as we grow.

What we stand for

Four commitments we do not negotiate on

These values guide how we work with our clients, look after our people, and serve the communities and province we come from.

Respect for PNG laws and professionalism

We respect the laws of Papua New Guinea, the constitution and our labour laws. We hold ourselves to professional, ethical standards in every engagement we take on.

Client satisfaction and quality output

We aim for client satisfaction and quality output delivered within the agreed time and budget. We want every client to feel they received fair value for what they paid.

Valuing our employees' time and effort

We value the time and effort of our staff, and we run an appraisal system that rewards performance so that good work is recognised and people can build a career with us.

Positive community development

We are committed to positive community development, especially in the lives of as many young people as we can reach in our local communities and across Milne Bay Province.
