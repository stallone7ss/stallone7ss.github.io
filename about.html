Our story

From two engineers and a spreadsheet

In 2014 our founders were senior engineers at larger firms, watching tickets bounce between three subcontracted providers while clients had no idea who actually owned their problem. The fix was unglamorous: one team, named engineers, published response times, and a monthly report that showed exactly what was happening.

That model still works. We now run 24/7 operations from three regions, but the promise has not changed: you will always know who is accountable, and you will always be able to see whether we hit our numbers.

Independently owned

Kilstar is employee-owned, with no vendor parent or resale quota. We are paid to reduce your risk, not to sell you hardware.

What we stand for

Four commitments we do not negotiate on

These are written into our employment contracts, our service agreements and our client reviews. They are the reason clients stay an average of 4.6 years.

Own the outcome, not the ticket

Closing a ticket is not the job — restoring the business is. Engineers stay with an issue until the user is genuinely unblocked, and we measure repeat contacts, not closures.

Show the numbers, including the bad ones

Every client gets a live dashboard. Missed SLAs are reported proactively with automatic credits. Hiding a bad month costs more than admitting it early.

Hire for judgement, not just certifications

Certificates get you an interview. Every engineer has 1,200 hours of supervised on-call before they take a ticket alone, and a senior engineer reviews their first ten tickets.

Secure by default, not by upsell

MFA, immutable backups and tested restores are included in every plan. We have never held a client hostage by making basic security a premium add-on.
