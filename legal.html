Every managed engagement carries a written SLA with response and resolution targets, measurement windows, exclusions and an automatic credit schedule. The summary below is the standard Professional-plan position; Enterprise plans are negotiated.

Response and resolution targets

SLA response and resolution targets by priority
Priority Example Response Resolution
P1 CriticalBusiness down site-wide15 min4 hrs
P2 HighMany users, no workaround1 hour8 hrs
P3 NormalSome users, workaround exists4 hours2 days
P4 RequestNew requirement, no outage1 day5 days

Uptime commitment

99.99% monthly availability for managed production systems, measured per calendar month excluding agreed maintenance windows. Availability is calculated as (total minutes − downtime minutes) ÷ total minutes.

Service credits

  • Missed P1 response: 5% of the monthly fee per affected system.
  • Uptime below 99.9%: 5% credit; below 99.0%: 10%; below 95.0%: 20%.
  • Missed monthly review: 5% credit.
  • Credits are applied automatically. You do not need to ask.

Reporting and review

Clients receive a live dashboard, a monthly service report and a quarterly business review. Service credits for a month are confirmed in the following month's report.

Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance notified at least 72 hours in advance, third-party carrier or utility failures, and outages caused by client equipment or configuration changes made outside our documented procedures.
