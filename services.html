Backup & Disaster Recovery

From $6 / endpoint / month

Backup is not a checkbox — it is a tested, documented recovery plan. We protect every workload, run immutable copies that ransomware cannot reach, and prove it by restoring on a schedule and handing you the evidence.

Coverage

  • Endpoint & server image backups
  • Microsoft 365, Google Workspace & SQL
  • VMware, Hyper-V and NAS/SAN volumes
  • SaaS & cloud-native workloads
  • Immutable, air-gapped vault copies
  • Recovery orchestration for full-site loss

Objectives

  • RPO — data loss window 15 minutes
  • RTO — time to restore 4 hours
  • Restore testing Monthly
  • Documentation Runbook, tested
